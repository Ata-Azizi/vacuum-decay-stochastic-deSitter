# Stochastic Approach to de Sitter Phase Transition and Interacting Fields

**Author:** Ata Azizi Seraji  
**Supervisors:** Prof. Laura Covi, Dr. Sarif Khan  
**Institution:** Institute for Theoretical Physics, University of Göttingen

---

## About

This poster summarizes the results of a research internship carried out at the Institute for Theoretical Physics, Göttingen, and was presented within the research group of Prof. Laura Covi.

It presents a stochastic approach to vacuum decay and phase transitions in de Sitter spacetime. The stochastic formalism, based on the Langevin and Fokker–Planck equations, provides an alternative to the standard semiclassical picture by treating long-wavelength field modes as stochastic variables driven by quantum noise from short-wavelength modes.

The vacuum decay rate is identified as the lowest non-zero eigenvalue of a Schrödinger-like equation derived from the Fokker–Planck equation. The poster also covers spatial and temporal correlation functions, perturbative eigenvalue expansions for interacting scalar fields with quartic potentials, and the treatment of both bounded and unbounded potential cases.

---

## Topics Covered

- Stochastic formalism in de Sitter spacetime
- Langevin and Fokker–Planck equations for scalar fields
- Spectral expansion and Schrödinger-like eigenvalue equation
- Spatial and temporal two-point correlation functions
- Interacting scalar fields with quartic potentials
- Vacuum decay rates in bounded and unbounded potentials
